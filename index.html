Why local matters

We work the South Plains every day.

Lubbock sits on the Llano Estacado at the edge of the South Plains cotton belt, where dry semi-arid summers give way to winters that can swing 30°F in a single afternoon when a cold front rolls in off the Caprock. Rodent pressure here doesn't behave like it does in East Texas or the Gulf Coast: roof rats move into attics on a tighter seasonal cycle, Norway rats hold steady year-round in the older neighborhoods around downtown and the Texas Tech medical district, and house mice spike sharply the week the first hard freeze lands.

Agricultural and oilfield activity changes the picture further. Properties on the cotton-belt edge see seasonal migration when fields are harvested. Oilfield service yards and pump-station sheds west of Lubbock pick up rodent pressure that suburban exterminators don't usually plan for. We've built programs around all of it: student rentals near Texas Tech, restaurants and warehouses along the Loop 289 corridor, storage facilities across the city, and the working agricultural and oilfield properties on the surrounding plains.

Know what you're dealing with

How we work

Four steps. No upsell theater.

1

Inspect

Walk the property: attic, crawl space, foundation, roof line, garage, outbuildings. We map every entry point, droppings location, and active runway before we propose anything.

2

Identify

Roof rat, Norway rat, or house mouse, each has a different treatment path. We confirm species from droppings, gnaw patterns, runway height, and nest material.

3

Treat

Targeted baiting, trapping, and removal calibrated to the species and infestation stage. Bait stations are tamper-resistant; trapping-only programs are available for pet households.

4

Prevent

Exclusion sealing, vent screening, and follow-up visits to confirm activity has stopped. Without this step, rodents come back, which is why many homeowners who call are looking for a second opinion on a first company's work.

Common questions

What Lubbock homeowners ask us most.

Do you offer same-day rodent service in Lubbock?
Yes. Same-day inspections are available across Lubbock and the South Plains towns we serve. Call (806) 207-3665 and we'll dispatch the next available technician, most same-day visits happen within four hours of the call.
Why do Lubbock homes get worse rodent problems when a cold front rolls through?
West Texas cold fronts can drop temperatures 30°F in a few hours. Roof rats and house mice respond by pushing toward warm interior spaces, attics, wall voids, garages, exactly where exclusion gaps tend to be. Activity reports spike in the 48 hours after a front passes.
Which rodents are most common in Lubbock?
Three species dominate calls in Lubbock: roof rats (heavy in older neighborhoods with mature trees and attic access), Norway rats (sewers, foundations, ground-level entry in older areas), and the house mouse (year-round, every neighborhood). Each requires a different treatment path.
Are your treatments safe around pets and children?
Yes. We use tamper-resistant bait stations and place trapping equipment in locations pets and children cannot reach. If you prefer to avoid baits entirely, ask about our trapping-only programs, common for homes with curious dogs.
How is a rodent control job quoted in Lubbock?
There is no flat rate. A residential rat or mouse program is quoted after the inspection and depends on the species, the size of the property, and how much attic or crawl-space exclusion is required. Commercial, agricultural, and oilfield properties are quoted separately. Inspections are free.
Do you serve nearby towns outside Lubbock city limits?
Yes. We service Wolfforth, Slaton, Levelland, Shallowater, Idalou, Abernathy, New Deal, Ransom Canyon, and 16 other South Plains towns within roughly 45 minutes of Lubbock. See our full service-area list for the complete coverage map.
