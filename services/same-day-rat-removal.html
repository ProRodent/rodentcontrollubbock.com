When a daytime same-day call is the right option in Lubbock.

Field-tested protocols.

Most rat infestations don't actually require emergency response, if the activity is in the attic and hasn't reached living spaces, a scheduled appointment works fine. Same-day calls are the right move when a live rat has been spotted inside the home and not only in the attic, when a cold front just hit and the homeowner can hear heavy attic activity for the first time, or when a property is being sold or rented and the inspection deadline creates urgency. We triage on the phone and dispatch accordingly.

Lubbock's cold-front cycle makes same-day capacity more important here than in most Texas cities. A major cold front can push many roof rats toward attic access points at once. A property that had zero rat activity yesterday can have audible attic activity the next morning. Getting in the same day, inspecting the access points, and deploying bait and emergency exclusion stops the colony from becoming established before the colony breeds.

What same-day rat removal includes.

Inspection, treatment, sealing, follow-up.

  • Same-day dispatch: priority scheduling for daytime calls; after-hours dispatch available for genuine emergencies.
  • Full inspection on arrival: attic, exterior perimeter, and interior if there is evidence of living-space entry. Species identified before treatment starts.
  • Immediate treatment deployment: bait stations and/or snap traps placed on confirmed runways during the same visit.
  • Emergency exclusion: primary entry point closed during the initial visit using permanent materials where possible.
  • Follow-up scheduled: full exclusion and program completion scheduled within 3-5 days of the same-day visit.

Frequently asked questions.

How fast can you get to me for same-day rat removal?
For calls received before 2 PM on most days, we can typically dispatch within 2-4 hours across Lubbock County. Calls received later in the afternoon are usually scheduled for the following morning unless there is a genuine emergency: live rat in the living space, dead-rat odor in HVAC, or similar.
Do you charge extra for same-day service?
Same-day scheduling does not carry a premium over our standard service rates. After-hours dispatch does carry an additional dispatch fee, which we quote on the phone before sending anyone.
Can you do same-day service in Wolfforth, Shallowater, or Slaton?
Yes: all three are within our standard same-day dispatch range. Most South Plains towns within 30 minutes of Lubbock qualify. Towns farther out may be next-day depending on schedule.
Will same-day service fully solve my rat problem?
The same-day visit handles the immediate situation: species ID, initial treatment, and primary entry point closure. Full exclusion and program completion typically happen on a follow-up visit 3-5 days later. The same-day visit stops the active incursion; the follow-up finishes the job.
What should I do before the technician arrives?
Note where you have seen activity, heard scratching, or found droppings. Do not try to block entry points yourself, improper sealing can trap rats inside the wall or attic, making removal harder. Leave attic access clear if you have a pull-down stair.
Is same-day service available on weekends?
Yes. We operate seven days a week including weekends. Weekend dispatch is available for both scheduled same-day and after-hours emergency calls.
Do you handle both roof rats and Norway rats on same-day calls?
Yes. We carry equipment and materials for both species. Species is confirmed on arrival from inspection evidence, and the treatment approach is set accordingly during the same visit.
