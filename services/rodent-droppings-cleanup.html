Rodent droppings cleanup is the safe removal and disinfection of rodent fecal matter, urine residue, and nesting material from affected areas using wet-treatment protocols that prevent aerosolization of hantavirus and other rodent-borne pathogens. In Lubbock, TX, droppings cleanup is most commonly needed in attics following a roof-rat infestation, in kitchen and pantry spaces after a mouse colony, and in crawl spaces where Norway rats have been active.

Why dry-sweeping droppings is dangerous.

Specific patterns. Repeating annually.

Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome is a serious respiratory illness transmitted by inhaling aerosolized particles from infected rodent droppings, urine, or nesting material. The CDC protocols for rodent droppings cleanup are specific: disinfect first with a bleach solution or EPA-registered disinfectant, let it soak for five minutes, then remove the wet material using gloves and a disposable cloth, never sweep dry. Disturbing dry droppings in an enclosed space like an attic or pantry cabinet is the primary exposure pathway.

Lubbock's South Plains region has documented hantavirus risk, it's not theoretical. Deer mice, which are the primary hantavirus reservoir, are present throughout West Texas, and while house mice and roof rats carry different pathogens (salmonella, leptospirosis, rat-bite fever), the correct cleanup protocol is the same regardless of species.

What rodent droppings cleanup includes.

Defined deliverables. No surprises mid-job.

  • PPE-protected removal: technician wears respirator (N95 minimum), disposable gloves, and coveralls. No bare-skin contact with contaminated surfaces.
  • Wet disinfection first: EPA-registered disinfectant applied to all contaminated surfaces and allowed to soak before any material is disturbed or removed.
  • Bagged disposal: all droppings, nesting material, and contaminated debris double-bagged and disposed of in outdoor waste containers.
  • Surface disinfection: all affected surfaces wiped down with disinfectant after removal. Attic insulation, pantry shelving, and cabinet interiors treated as appropriate.
  • Contamination extent assessment: if HVAC ducts, insulation voids, or wall cavities show signs of contamination, scope is expanded and documented.

Frequently asked questions.

Is it safe to clean up rodent droppings myself?
It can be done safely if you follow the correct protocol: wet the droppings with disinfectant first, wait five minutes, then remove with gloves and a disposable cloth while wearing an N95 respirator. Do not sweep dry. Do not vacuum without HEPA filtration. If the contamination is in an enclosed space like an attic or crawl space, professional cleanup is strongly recommended.
What diseases can rodent droppings carry?
House mice and rats can carry salmonella, leptospirosis, and rat-bite fever. Deer mice, which are common in rural West Texas areas around Lubbock, are the primary reservoir for Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome. The correct cleanup protocol applies regardless of which species was present.
Does my attic insulation need to be replaced after a rat infestation?
Not always. If droppings are concentrated in specific areas and the insulation hasn't been heavily soiled or compressed by nesting, targeted cleanup and disinfection may be enough. We assess the insulation condition and give you an honest recommendation. If replacement is needed, we quote it separately.
Do you clean HVAC ducts that may have been contaminated?
Duct cleaning for rodent damage is a specialized service. We assess whether the HVAC system has been accessed by rodents and, if so, refer you to an appropriate duct cleaning service. We treat the surrounding areas and note any duct access points that need to be sealed.
How long does droppings cleanup take?
A standard kitchen or pantry cleanup takes 1-2 hours. Attic cleanup depends on the colony extent and the attic's physical conditions, typical residential attics run 2-4 hours. We give a time and cost estimate after the initial assessment.
Do you clean up droppings in storage units or outbuildings?
Yes. Storage units, sheds, garages, and outbuildings are within our standard cleanup scope. Pricing depends on the size of the space and the extent of contamination.
Is cleanup included in the treatment program?
The initial treatment visit includes removal of accessible carcasses. Droppings cleanup is a separate service, typically scheduled after the colony has been confirmed eliminated. We can schedule both in sequence.
