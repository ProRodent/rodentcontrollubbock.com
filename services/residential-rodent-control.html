Residential rodent control is a whole-home rat and mouse management program covering inspection, species identification, interior and exterior treatment, structural exclusion sealing, and follow-up verification for single-family residences. In Lubbock, TX, residential programs are the core of our route: the South Plains housing mix of brick-veneer construction, older slab-on-grade homes, and newer suburban builds on the city's edges each presents a different set of entry-point vulnerabilities.

What residential rodent pressure looks like in Lubbock.

Inspection-first approach.

The most common residential call we get in Lubbock follows a pattern: homeowner hears scratching in the attic or wall, buys hardware-store traps, catches one or two animals, and then calls us six weeks later when the activity hasn't stopped. The problem is almost never the trap. The entry point was never found or sealed. The colony replacement cycle is faster than the trap-and-wait approach.

Lubbock's brick-veneer housing is particularly prone to weep-hole entry for house mice. The weep holes, the small gaps in the mortar at the base of brick construction, are there by design for moisture drainage, but they're exactly the right size for a house mouse, and most pest companies don't address them. Roof rats come in higher up: soffit vents, gable vents, the junction between the fascia board and roof sheathing where wood has shrunk over time. We find and seal both categories as part of the standard residential program.

What this service includes.

Every step gets documented.

  • Full home inspection: attic, crawl space (if present), interior wall voids, kitchen and pantry, garage, and full exterior perimeter from foundation to roof line.
  • Species ID: confirms whether the home has rats, mice, or both, and which species of rat is present.
  • Interior treatment: snap traps on confirmed runways, bait stations where appropriate, attic baiting for roof-rat infestations.
  • Exterior exclusion: weep-hole mesh, soffit-vent screening, gable-vent hardware cloth, door-sweep replacement, utility-penetration sealing.
  • Follow-up visit: return at 10-14 days to confirm activity has stopped and close any secondary entry points.
  • Optional quarterly program: ongoing perimeter monitoring and exclusion maintenance for properties in high-pressure neighborhoods.

Frequently asked questions.

What's the most common entry point for rodents in Lubbock homes?
For house mice: weep holes in brick veneer, gaps around utility pipes, and worn door sweeps. For roof rats: soffit vents, gable vents, and the junction between fascia boards and roof sheathing where wood has shrunk over time. We find and seal both categories on every residential call.
How long does a residential treatment take?
The initial inspection and treatment visit takes 1.5-3 hours depending on property size. The follow-up visit at 10-14 days typically takes 30-60 minutes.
Do you treat the attic on residential calls?
Yes, for any call with suspected roof-rat activity. Attic inspection and baiting are standard for roof-rat programs. For mouse-only calls, the attic is still inspected as part of the full perimeter check.
How is residential rodent control quoted in Lubbock?
A single-family program is quoted from the size of the home, the species present, and the number of entry points that need sealing. Older homes with more exclusion work take more time. Inspections are free.
Do you offer recurring programs?
Yes. For properties in high-pressure neighborhoods, Tech Terrace, Heart of Lubbock, older blocks near downtown, we offer optional quarterly perimeter checks and exclusion maintenance.
Is your service safe for a home with young children?
Yes. Bait stations are tamper-resistant. Snap traps are placed in locations children can't access (inside cabinets, against wall bases behind appliances). We'll walk you through what we've placed and where before we leave.
What if the rodents come back after treatment?
Recurrence after a properly executed exclusion program is rare. If it happens within 30 days of our follow-up sign-off, we reinspect at no charge to find the secondary entry point that was missed.
Can you treat my home the same day I call?
Same-day inspections are available for most Lubbock neighborhoods. Call (806) 207-3665 and we'll confirm same-day availability when you call.
Do you serve all of Lubbock?
Yes: all 31 Lubbock neighborhoods, from Arnett Benson to Windsor Park, plus 24 nearby South Plains towns.
