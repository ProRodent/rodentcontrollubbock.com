Common questions from Maxey Park homeowners about rodent control.

Is Maxey Park one of the highest-pressure roof-rat areas in Lubbock?
Yes. The combination of the park's mature canopy and the neighborhood's older construction makes it one of the most consistent service areas for roof-rat attic calls.
How close to the park do I need to live for this to be a concern?
Properties within 2-3 blocks of the park perimeter have the heaviest pressure. Properties farther into the neighborhood still have roof-rat risk from the established lot trees, but the intensity is lower.
Do you recommend preventive exclusion in Maxey Park?
Yes. Preventive roof-line exclusion in August or September is the most effective approach for Maxey Park properties, given the consistency of cold-front-season incursions.
What does attic cleanup typically involve in this neighborhood?
For established multi-season infestations, cleanup includes nest removal, droppings removal with wet disinfection, surface disinfection, and insulation assessment. Insulation replacement is recommended in cases with extensive urine saturation or heavy compression.

Yes. Wire chewing is flagged on every attic inspection. Given the colony ages we commonly find in this neighborhood, wiring assessment is particularly important.
