Ballenger: trash cans and sanitation

The Texas A&M guide recommends that trash cans sit at least 30 feet from buildings with lids closed, that compactor chutes be kept clean and in good repair, and that food trash not collect along foundations.

Pet food, bird seed and fallen fruit are the same kind of food source. Store them in sealed containers and clear them nightly during an active problem.

What we look for on the roofline

From the ground and then from inside the attic, we check soffits, gable vents, flashing and places where pipes pass through the roof. Roof rats use branches and wires to reach these points, so we also note anything touching the roof of the Ballenger property.

Questions about rodent control in Ballenger

How far should trash cans be from the building?
At least 30 feet, with lids closed, per the Texas A&M guide.
Does pet food attract rodents?
Yes. Any accessible food is a food source, so store it in sealed containers.

Pet food and bird seed

Pet food left out overnight and spilled bird seed feed rodents as well as pets. According to Texas A&M, removing food sources is part of integrated rodent management, not an extra step.

Store bags in sealed metal or hard plastic bins, and pick up dishes at night.

Source for "Pet food and bird seed" (published guidelines): Texas A&M AgriLife Extension, Rodent IPM.
