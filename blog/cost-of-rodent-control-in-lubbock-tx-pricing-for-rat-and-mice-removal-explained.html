Cost of Rodent Control in Lubbock, TX: What Drives the Quote

By Lubbock Rodent Control · Updated November 17, 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Four variables drive the quote: Species involved, colony extent, property size, and exclusion scope. These determine the final quote, not a flat-rate formula.
  • Inspections are free for treatment customers: You don't pay for the assessment. The quote is given before any work starts.
  • Quotes are itemized: Treatment, exclusion, cleanup, and insulation work appear as separate line items, so you can see what each part covers.
  • Exclusion is the variable that swings the quote most: Entry-point count and materials change the exclusion scope more than any other factor.
  • No surprise invoices: Every quote is confirmed before work begins. After-hours emergency dispatch carries a dispatch fee on top of treatment, quoted on the phone before we head out.

What a rodent control job costs in Lubbock, TX depends on four variables: the species involved, the colony extent, the property size, and the amount of exclusion work required. We do not publish a price list, because two homes on the same street can need very different work. This guide explains what each part of a quote covers, what pushes it up or down, and how to compare quotes from different providers.

Free inspections vs. paid inspections.

Inspections for customers scheduling treatment service are always free. A standalone written report, such as a pre-purchase inspection, documentation for a real estate transaction, or a report for an insurance claim, is quoted separately based on property size and report scope. If you are not sure you have a rodent problem and want an expert opinion before committing to anything, the initial assessment visit is free.

What shapes an exclusion quote.

Pricing reflects property reality.

Exclusion is quoted separately from treatment because the scope varies widely between properties. An older Lubbock home in Tech Terrace or Overton may have many weep holes, several worn soffit vents, and open fascia gaps, which makes a full exclusion job a half-day of work. A newer home in Wolfforth may need only weep-hole sealing and a door sweep replacement, about 90 minutes of work. We quote exclusion after the inspection, from the actual entry-point count and the materials the job requires.

What shapes an attic cleanup and insulation quote.

Property-specific. Not a flat menu.

Attic cleanup after a roof-rat infestation is separate from treatment and exclusion. It is needed when the colony has been active long enough to leave droppings, nesting, and possible insulation damage. Cleanup scope follows attic size and colony extent. Insulation replacement, when warranted, is quoted by attic square footage and covers removal of the soiled material and blown-in replacement. We confirm the target R-value with you before we quote.

What's included and what's not.

Every program we quote includes the initial treatment visit, a follow-up at 10-14 days, and all treatment materials. Exclusion sealing is quoted separately and done during the treatment visit or on a concurrent exclusion visit. Attic cleanup, insulation replacement, and ongoing commercial service rotations are separate line items. There are no surprise invoices: the quote confirmed before work starts is the amount billed at completion.

The real cost comparison: A homeowner who pays for annual rat treatment without exclusion for five years usually spends more than one who pays for treatment and exclusion once, because exclusion closes the entry points that bring rodents back. For most Lubbock homes, closing those entry points is what ends the yearly repeat.

What a low quote might mean.

A quote far below what other providers offer for a full-service program usually means one of three things: an inspection only (no treatment), a single treatment visit without follow-up or exclusion, or a program that uses consumer-grade materials. Ask specifically what the quote includes before accepting it.

What a high quote might mean.

A quote that looks high for a standard single-family program (treatment plus exclusion, no cleanup) deserves a closer look. A large older home with extensive exclusion work can legitimately cost more, but you should still be able to see why. Ask for a line-item breakdown that shows what is being quoted.

Comparing quotes from different providers.

When comparing quotes, make sure you are comparing the same scope. The key questions are: (1) Does the quote include follow-up visits? (2) Is exclusion included or quoted separately? (3) What materials are used for exclusion (foam and caulk, or hardware cloth and copper mesh)? Two quotes that look similar can be very different programs. A quote that includes follow-up and copper-mesh weep-hole sealing is a better value than a cheaper one that is a single treatment visit with caulk-only exclusion.

Frequently asked questions.

Does rodent inspection cost anything in Lubbock?
For customers scheduling treatment service, the inspection is always free. Standalone written reports for real estate or insurance purposes are quoted separately.
Why does exclusion cost more on older homes?
Older homes have more entry points: more weep holes (both floors), degraded soffit vent screening, open fascia gaps, more utility penetrations that have aged caulk. A home with 80 weep holes requires far more copper mesh and time than one with 40.
Is attic cleanup always required after a rat infestation?
Not always. If the colony was early-stage and quickly treated, cleanup may not be warranted. We assess honestly at the cleanup visit and recommend replacement only when the contamination or compression level warrants it.
Do you charge extra for same-day service?
Same-day scheduling carries no premium. After-hours emergency dispatch (evening/weekend) carries an additional dispatch fee, quoted on the phone before dispatch.
What's the cheapest way to deal with a Lubbock mouse infestation?
Early treatment, before the colony establishes. A single-visit mouse program for an early-stage infestation is the smallest scope we quote. Waiting until the colony is established and present in multiple rooms widens the scope and adds a multi-visit program requirement.

Why a written scope matters when comparing quotes

Texas Department of Agriculture rules require a structural pest control business to hold a license for each location and to maintain general liability insurance, and the business must print a TDA jurisdiction statement on contracts. When you compare quotes, those documents are worth asking about alongside the scope of work.

Texas A&M AgriLife Extension adds that rodent work is rarely a single step. Trapping takes near-daily attention and suits small populations, while sealing every opening is what keeps a colony from returning. A quote that lists the inspection, the treatment method, the entry points to be sealed, and the follow-up visits is easier to compare than one that lists a single line item.
